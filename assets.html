On The Lead Company That Did Not Work Out
This is worth saying before we talk about anything else

You told us a lead company three years ago was not satisfactory. With this setup, there is no version of that engagement where either side could have proved what happened. There was no record of which enquiry came from where, so a good month and a bad month look identical afterwards.

That is not a defence of them. It is a warning about hiring anyone next, including us. If you take on a marketing company while nothing is measuring, you are buying a story rather than a service. Ask us — or whoever else you speak to — how they intend to prove it worked, and do not accept traffic or impressions as the answer.

Tracking first. Then spend. It is a small job, it belongs to you rather than to us, and it is the only thing that makes the next twelve months measurable.

The Order We Would Do It In
  • Move the site onto all4roofs.com properly. Nothing else matters while your own address hands visitors to a staging URL. This is settings, not a rebuild.
  • Install GA4 and Google Ads conversion tracking before another dollar runs. Calls and forms counted as conversions, not clicks.
  • Put a tappable phone number on every page. Commercial buyers call; they do not fill in forms and wait.
  • Write a real meta description, add the canonical, cut to one H1. An afternoon.
  • Publish structured data. Front Range Roofing does not have it — this is a place you can lead the field outright, for free.
  • Then build the commercial and multifamily pages into the strongest on the site, written for property managers and HOA boards rather than for homeowners.

Nobody in Denver commercial roofing can prove what works. You have the most history to prove it with.
