Finding 1 — The Redirect
REQUEST https://all4roofs.com/ RESPONSE 301 Moved Permanently LOCATION https://all4roofs.wpcomstaging.com/ REQUEST https://www.all4roofs.com/ RESPONSE 301 -> https://all4roofs.com/ -> then the staging address Both checked directly on 28 September 2026.
Why this is the first thing on the list

A 301 is a permanent redirect. It tells Google and every browser that the staging address is the real home of this website, forever. So the address on your van, your radio ad and your business cards hands its visitors — and all of its search authority — to a URL with someone else's product name in it.

Every person who clicks one of your ads sees it. They type or click all4roofs.com and land on all4roofs.wpcomstaging.com. Some will not notice. The ones spending forty thousand dollars on a commercial re-roof are exactly the ones who will.

This is a settings fix, not a rebuild. The site itself is fine. It is pointed at the wrong front door, and it has been for long enough that Google has learned the wrong one.

Finding 2 — Two Years Of Ads, And Nothing Counting

This is the expensive one, and it is why the last lead company felt like a waste

Jetpack counts visits. It does not count calls, forms or jobs, and it cannot tell Google Ads which click produced work. So for two years the platform has been optimising towards whatever it could see, which is clicks, not customers.

You told us a lead company three years ago did not work out. Here is the uncomfortable part: with this setup, neither you nor they could have proved it either way. There was no record of which enquiry came from where. That is worth knowing before you hire anyone else, including us.

We would not run ads for you in this state and we would want the tracking live before any budget moved. It is a small job and it is the difference between spending and investing.

Finding 3 — A Commercial Buyer Cannot Call You From Your Own Website
CLICK-TO-CALL LINKS ON THE HOMEPAGE All4Roof (you) 0 Valor Roof and Solar present Front Range Roofing present Turner Morris none Enquiry forms on your homepage: 2 (these do work)
Forms are for homeowners. Commercial buyers phone.

A property manager with a leaking multifamily roof does not fill in a form and wait. They call the first contractor who makes it easy. On a phone, a number that is not a tappable link is a number that has to be written down first.

Your forms are working and that is genuinely good — two of them, on the homepage. But the highest-value enquiry you get is a phone call, and the site does not invite one.

Finding 4 — The Page Is Telling Google Very Little
Title "all-4-roofing - The Best Roofing In Colorado" 50 chars Description "The Best Roofing In Colorado" 27 chars H1 tags 2 (there should be one) H2 tags 2 (Front Range Roofing has 26) Canonical absent Schema absent ALSO LINKED FROM THE HOMEPAGE /about-page-4 an unedited WordPress default slug /feed /comments/feed /amp /_static /context.item.anchor /context.submission.url template placeholders
Small things, but they are the cheap ones

“The Best Roofing In Colorado” is your entire meta description. That is the sentence Google shows under your name in the results. It says nothing about commercial, multifamily, where you work or why to call. Twenty-seven characters where you are allowed about a hundred and fifty.

Two H1s confuses the page's own subject, and /about-page-4 is the slug WordPress generates when nobody renames it. Neither is fatal. Both are free to fix and both are visible to anyone checking you out.

Why we are not proposing a rebuild

The structure underneath is better than the score suggests. You have commercial and multifamily pages already, which is the hard part and the part that matches the work worth having.

What is missing is the front door, the measuring, and the phone. None of that requires starting again.

The Order We Would Do It In
  • Move the site onto all4roofs.com properly and stop the redirect to staging. Nothing else matters until your own address serves your own website.
  • Install GA4 and Google Ads conversion tracking before another dollar of budget runs. Calls and forms counted, not clicks.
  • Put a tappable phone number on every page, top and bottom.
  • Write a real meta description, add the canonical tag, and cut the page to one H1.
  • Add structured data — name, address, phone, service area. Two of your three competitors publish it and you publish none.
  • Then build out the commercial and multifamily pages you already have into the strongest pages on the site, because that is where the money is.

Two years of spend, arriving at a staging address, with nothing counting it.

Fix those three things and everything you are already paying for starts working.
