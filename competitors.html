Read the four rightmost columns of your own row

No canonical, no schema, no analytics, no phone link. You are last or joint-last on every structural measure on this table, and you are the only firm with two H1 tags and a twenty-seven character description.

This is not about effort. You are the one buying radio and running ads for two years. It is about a website that was left on its staging settings and never finished.

Finding 1 — Front Range Is Winning On Structure, Not Spend
Front Range Roofing 1,722 words · 26 H2 subheadings · 138KB frontrangeroofing.com/service-areas/commercial-roofing-denver/ GA4 + Google Tag Manager · canonical · click-to-call no structured data · no ads conversion tag
Twenty-six subheadings is the whole trick, and it is not a clever one

They have a page built specifically for “commercial roofing Denver” with twenty-six sections on it. Each subheading answers a different question a commercial buyer has. That is why it ranks — not budget, not authority, just a page that covers the subject properly.

And they have no schema. Which means the thing they are beating you on is entirely writing, and the thing you could beat them on is free.

Finding 2 — Valor Is The One Directly In Your Lane
Valor Roof and Solar 1,669 words · 5 H2 · canonical · schema · tel valorroofandsolar.com/commercial-roofing/multifamily/ A dedicated MULTIFAMILY page -- the exact work your /multifamily-pca page is about.
They built a page for the buyer. You built a page for the service.

You have a multifamily page. So do they. The difference is theirs is written to an HOA board member or a property manager, sits at a clean URL with the word multifamily in it, publishes structured data and offers a tappable phone number.

Yours is called /multifamily-pca. That means something internally to you and nothing to a buyer or a search engine. Same asset, different execution.

Finding 3 — Turner Morris Shows What Authority Buys You
Turner Morris 603 words · 0 H1 tags · 12 H2 · canonical · schema · GA4 turnermorris.com no phone link
This one should encourage you

Fewer words than you, no H1 at all, and no click-to-call. They hold position on brand and history rather than on a well-built page.

Which means a properly finished site can outrank a bigger name here. Turner Morris is not beating you on execution. On the two things that are pure execution — words and headline structure — you are already level or ahead.

Finding 4 — Nobody In This Market Can Prove Anything
GOOGLE ADS CONVERSION TAG (AW-) All4Roof (you) none -- and you have advertised for 2 years Front Range Roofing none Valor Roof and Solar none Turner Morris none Four firms. Zero conversion tracking. Including you.
This is the opening, and it is the cheapest advantage on the table

Every one of these firms is spending money and guessing. Front Range has Tag Manager installed and still no conversion tag, which means even the most technically organised competitor here cannot tell you which page produced a commercial enquiry.

You have two years of ad history. The moment tracking goes on, that history starts producing answers instead of invoices — and you would be the only firm in this comparison who knows which words bring in a multifamily job.

What This Field Tells Us To Do
  • Match Turner Morris first, not Front Range. 603 words and no H1 is the bar for holding a position here. You are already at 753. Fix the structure and you are past them.
  • Rename and rewrite the multifamily page for the buyer, not the service code. Valor has shown exactly what that page should be.
  • Copy Front Range's subheading depth on one page only — commercial roofing Denver. Twenty-six sections answering twenty-six buyer questions. That is a writing job.
  • Publish structured data. Front Range does not have it. It is free and it is one of the few places you can lead this field outright.
  • Install conversion tracking and be the only firm in Denver commercial roofing that can prove what works. All four of you are blind today.

Nobody in this market is measuring anything. You have the most ad history to measure.

That combination is worth more to you than it would be to any of them.
